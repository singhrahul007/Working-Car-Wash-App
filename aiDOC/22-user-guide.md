# User Guide

## Overview

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for using the Car Wash Application. It covers everything from installation and setup to advanced features and troubleshooting.

## Getting Started

### Installation

#### Mobile Applications

**iOS:**
1. Open the App Store on your iOS device
2. Search for "Car Wash App"
3. Tap "Get" or "Install"
4. Wait for the installation to complete
5. Open the app and create an account

**Android:**
1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android device
2. Search for "Car Wash App"
3. Tap "Install"
4. Wait for the installation to complete
5. Open the app and create an account

#### Web Application
1. Navigate to https://carwashapp.com
2. Click "Sign Up" or "Get Started"
3. Create your account using email or social login
4. Verify your email address if required

### Account Setup

1. **Create Account**
   - Enter your email address
   - Set a secure password
   - Provide your name and phone number
   - Accept the terms of service

2. **Verify Email**
   - Check your inbox for verification email
   - Click the verification link
   - Confirm your account is active

3. **Complete Profile**
   - Add your vehicle information
   - Set preferred payment methods
   - Configure notification preferences

## Main Features

### Booking Services

#### Finding Providers
1. Open the app and navigate to "Find Providers"
2. Use location services to find nearby providers
3. Filter by:
   - Distance (1, 5, 10 miles)
   - Rating (4+, 3+, etc.)
   - Service categories
   - Availability

#### Selecting Services
1. Browse available services for each provider
2. View service details including:
   - Price and duration
   - Description and features
   - Reviews and ratings
3. Compare different providers' offerings

#### Making a Booking
1. Select desired service and time slot
2. Review booking details:
   - Date and time
   - Service type
   - Provider information
   - Total cost
3. Confirm booking with payment method

### Managing Bookings

#### Viewing Bookings
1. Navigate to "My Bookings"
2. See all current and past bookings
3. Filter by status (pending, confirmed, completed, cancelled)

#### Modifying Bookings
1. Select a booking from the list
2. View booking details and options
3. Change date/time if available
4. Cancel booking (if allowed)

#### Payment Management
1. Access payment history
2. View receipts for past bookings
3. Update payment methods
4. Set up recurring payments

### Provider Information

#### Provider Profiles
1. Browse provider ratings and reviews
2. View service offerings
3. Check location and contact information
4. See photos of the facility

#### Reviews and Ratings
1. Read reviews from other users
2. Rate your own experience
3. Leave feedback about services
4. Report issues or concerns

## Step-by-Step Tutorials

### How to Book a Service

1. **Open the App**
   - Launch the Car Wash App on your device

2. **Select Location**
   - Allow location access when prompted
   - Or manually enter your address

3. **Choose Service Type**
   - Browse available service categories:
     - Basic Wash
     - Premium Wash
     - Interior Cleaning
     - Detailing Services
   - Select the service that meets your needs

4. **Select Provider**
   - View nearby providers with their ratings
   - Filter by price, distance, or availability
   - Choose a provider you trust

5. **Schedule Appointment**
   - Select date and time that works for you
   - Review total cost and duration
   - Confirm appointment details

6. **Complete Payment**
   - Select preferred payment method
   - Enter payment information
   - Finalize booking confirmation

### How to Cancel a Booking

1. **Navigate to My Bookings**
   - Open the app and go to "My Bookings"

2. **Select Booking to Cancel**
   - Find the specific booking you want to cancel
   - Tap on the booking details

3. **Initiate Cancellation**
   - Look for "Cancel Booking" button
   - Confirm cancellation request

4. **Review Refund Policy**
   - Check if refund is possible
   - Note any cancellation fees that may apply

5. **Confirm Cancellation**
   - Complete the cancellation process
   - Receive confirmation message

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### App Won't Open
1. Check if your device meets minimum requirements
2. Restart your device
3. Update the app to latest version
4. Clear app cache if available

#### Location Services Not Working
1. Enable location services on your device
2. Grant app permission to access location
3. Try manual location entry
4. Ensure GPS is enabled

#### Booking Issues
1. Check provider availability
2. Verify payment method is valid
3. Confirm booking details are correct
4. Contact support if issue persists

#### Payment Problems
1. Ensure card is valid and has sufficient funds
2. Check for network connectivity
3. Try different payment methods
4. Contact your bank if payment is declined

### Contact Support

If you encounter issues not covered in this guide:

1. **In-App Support**
   - Use the "Help" or "Support" section within the app
   - Submit a support ticket with detailed information

2. **Email Support**
   - Send email to support@carwashapp.com
   - Include your account details and issue description

3. **Phone Support**
   - Call 1-800-CAR-WASH (1-800-227-9247)
   - Available Monday-Friday, 9AM-6PM
